Quick answers

Things people ask

The button does not buzz

Haptics need a real phone; simulators and the web preview show the button but do not vibrate. On iPhone, check Settings > Sounds & Haptics and make sure haptics are on. On Android, check that vibration is not turned off in your sound or accessibility settings. Then open Touchward's settings and pick a pattern: choosing one plays it, so you can compare.

Reminders are not showing up

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so the app needs notification permission. On iPhone, they can be delivered as time-sensitive so they break through Focus modes; you can allow that in Settings > Notifications > Touchward. On Android, some phones pause notifications for apps in the background under battery optimization. Excluding Touchward from battery optimization usually fixes it.

Can I trigger a tap without opening the app?

Yes. The link dopamine://reward fires a tap when it opens the app. On iPhone, make a Shortcut that opens that URL and assign it to the Action Button or Back Tap. On Android, use Quick Tap or a button remapper that can open a link. While the app is open, you can also turn on the volume buttons as a trigger in settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your settings and your tap count are stored on the phone. If you delete the app, they are gone, and there is no copy anywhere else.

I bought it on one phone. Can I use it on another?

Yes, on any phone signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account. Settings do not sync between phones, because nothing leaves the phone.

Privacy policy

Nothing leaves your phone

Effective September 14, 2026. Applies to the Touchward app and this website.

The short version

Touchward collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no server of ours that the app talks to. Everything the app needs is stored on your phone and stays there. This website sets no cookies. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to send us through the support form or by email.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your settings: the button shape, colors, haptic patterns, hold timer length, reminder schedule, and whether the volume buttons act as a trigger.
  • Your counter: the number of rewards today and in total.

This is kept in the app's private storage on the device. It is not sent anywhere, not backed up by us, and is deleted when you delete the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it under the terms of that service.

Phone features the app uses

  • Haptics and vibration. The app uses your phone's built-in vibration motor to play the reward patterns. On Android this needs the vibrate permission. Using the motor records nothing.
  • Notifications, only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone itself. There is no push service and no notification token is sent to us or anyone else. On iPhone, reminders can be marked time-sensitive so they break through Focus modes; you control this in the phone's notification settings. Turning reminders off or denying the permission stops them.
  • Volume buttons, only if you opt in. While the app is open, either volume button can count as a tap. The app does not record button presses beyond incrementing your counter.
  • The link dopamine://reward. Opening it fires a tap. It carries no data and nothing is logged.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, so no name, email, or password is ever collected by the app.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No crash or usage reporting from the app to us.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo, or health access. The app does not request them.
  • No in-app purchases and no data sold or shared with anyone, for any purpose.

The app stores

You buy Touchward through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Those companies handle the purchase and may collect their own data, such as payment details, download counts, and crash reports, under their privacy policies. We see aggregate download and sales numbers from them, never anything that identifies you. Refund requests go to the store, not to us.

This website and the support form

The site is static and sets no cookies. It has no analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google's servers; Google's privacy policy covers that request. When you use the support form we receive what you type (name if you give it, email, phone type, topic, message), the page the form was on, and your browser's user agent string, which helps us reproduce bugs. The message is delivered to our inbox by Resend, an email service. We use your email only to reply. We keep support messages as long as needed to resolve them and for our records, and delete them on request.

Children

Touchward is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because there is no account and no data collection, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete beyond uninstalling the app.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes your settings and counter. There is no copy anywhere else. If you have written to support and want that message deleted, email us and we will remove it.

Open source

The app's source code is public at github.com/situatedstrategies/dopamine-app, so every claim on this page can be checked against the code.
