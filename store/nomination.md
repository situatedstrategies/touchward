## Description

Touchward is a button you tap when you've done something. You get a buzz and a ripple back, and that's it. I made it because I wanted a small physical payoff for finishing things, without a streak to keep up or a feed to scroll. It's a paid app with no account and no ads, and nothing leaves the phone.

The button is the whole app. Pick a shape (the default is the wavy ring from the icon) and the hold timer traces that outline while you press. Each tap sends a ripple out in the same shape. Seven reward modes pair a motion with a haptic. The haptics are real Core Haptics patterns, with five strengths and over 150 presets to choose from. Colors come from a color wheel with hex entry, and Random builds a new look every press.

Reminders are local. Calendar nudges go off when a meeting ends, from the calendar already on the phone. There's an Apple Watch app with the same button, and the touchward://reward link works with the Action Button and Back Tap.
