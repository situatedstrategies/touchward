## Description (4000)

Touchward is a sensory reward button. You finish something, you open the
app, you tap, and you get a small satisfying buzz and a ripple of light back.
That is the whole idea. It is a tiny ritual for closing a task, marking a
win, or just giving yourself a moment.

Tap it. Or hold it: a timer traces the outline of the button while you
press, and when it completes you get a bigger reward. Choose tap only, hold
only, or both.

Keep score

- Every tap counts. A big counter at the top of the screen shows today's
  taps and how many are left until the next milestone.
- Every 500 taps, all time, the button goes berserk: for a few seconds your
  phone buzzes like crazy while the button shakes, pops, and runs through
  random colors and ripples. Then it settles down and the countdown starts
  again.
- Your all time total lives in Customize, More, along with the next
  milestone. Reset it whenever you want a fresh run.

Make it yours

- Shapes: circle, the icon's wavy ring, squircle, square, hexagon, star,
  heart, or blob. Every shape glows, and the hold timer follows its outline.
- Ripples: each tap sends a ripple out from the button. Pick the ripple's
  outline, its thickness, how long it lingers before it fades, and how many
  rings can stack at once.
- Reward modes: Original (three standing rings that a wave rolls through),
  Ripple, Soft, Spark, Deep, Double, and Wave. Each sets the motion and the
  haptic together.
- Colors: a full color picker with a hue wheel, brightness slider, and hex
  entry for the button, its reward colors, the three ripple colors, and the
  background. Save colors you like to a library under each setting.
- Feel: five haptic strengths, from Gentle to Max, and on supported phones a
  library of over 150 haptic presets to put on a tap or on the timer.
- Random: one press builds a completely new look. Save the ones you love and
  keep them in your library.

Reminders that fit your day

- Calendar nudges: with calendar access, Touchward notices when each event
  ends and nudges you to tap, right away or a few minutes after. The meeting
  is over, the workout is done, come tap.
- Reminders: a schedule you set, so many hours apart between the hours you
  choose.
- Time sensitive delivery on iPhone, so a nudge can reach you in a Focus mode.

Tap from anywhere

- Volume buttons: while the app is open, either volume button counts as a tap.
- A link, touchward://reward, fires a tap when it opens the app. Wire it to
  the Action Button or Back Tap through Shortcuts, or to Quick Tap on Android.
- Apple Watch: the button and its ripples on your wrist, with your reminders
  mirrored, and taps counted alongside the phone.

Private by design

No account and nothing to sign in to. Your settings and your counter stay on
your phone. Calendar data is read only to schedule nudges and is never stored
or sent. The app never asks for your email or your name. If it crashes, an
anonymous report helps us fix it, and you can turn that off.

Touchward is free to download: 100 rewards a day with the default look, and
no ads. Touchward Full is one in-app purchase of 0.49, once: no daily limit
and every shape, color, mode and haptic. No subscription.

Google Play Data safety form. Google counts data as "collected" only when
it leaves the device, so calendar events, the counter, and the settings are
not listed; a transfer to a service provider acting for us (Resend, Firebase,
RevenueCat, Expo's push service) is "collected", not "shared".

- Overview: collects user data, yes. Encrypted in transit, yes (HTTPS).
  Deletion request mechanism, no: nothing collected is tied to a person or
  an account, so there is nothing to find and delete; uninstalling removes
  everything on the device. No account creation.
- Crash logs (App info and performance): collected, not shared, optional
  (Customize, More, "Send crash reports"), purpose App functionality. Error
  text, stack, app version, device model, OS version, the look settings.
- App interactions (App activity): collected, not shared, optional
  ("Share usage analytics"), purpose Analytics. Google Analytics for
  Firebase events: rewards, unlocks, review prompts, sessions, screens.
- Device or other IDs: collected, not shared, required, purposes App
  functionality and Analytics. The RevenueCat app user id (random, created at
  launch for the purchase), the Firebase app instance id (reset when
  analytics is turned off), and the device push token sent to Expo's push
  service only with push turned on. "Does your app use advertising ID?": no.
  The AD_ID permission is blocked in app.json and the Firebase Analytics
  advertising ID collection is turned off by manifest meta-data
  (plugins/withNoAdvertisingId.js), so no SDK in the build reads it.
- Purchase history (Financial info): collected, not shared, required,
  purpose App functionality. The App Store or Play receipt, through
  RevenueCat, which confirms and restores the purchase.
- Other in-app messages (Messages): collected, not shared, optional,
  purpose App functionality. What is typed into the support form, with the
  device model and app version.
- Not collected: name, email, location, calendar events, photos, contacts,
  files, health data. Google Play Games sign-in is run by Google Play
  services; the app shows the player name and avatar on the device and
  sends neither anywhere.
- No Advertising or marketing purpose anywhere on the form, and in App
  content, Ads: "No, my app does not contain ads".

  any locked choice, from Random, and on the first attempt past the day's
  100th reward. Its copy lives in the RevenueCat dashboard, not in this
  repository, so rewrite it by hand: title "Touchward Full", one line "No
