## Description (4000)

Touchward is a sensory reward button. You finish something, you open the
app, you tap, and you get a small satisfying buzz and a ripple of light back.
That is the whole idea. It is a tiny ritual for closing a task, marking a
win, or just giving yourself a moment.

Tap it. Or hold it: a timer traces the outline of the button while you
press, and when it completes you get a bigger reward. Choose tap only, hold
only, or both.

Make it yours

- Shapes: circle, the icon's wavy ring, squircle, square, hexagon, star,
  heart, or blob. Every shape glows, and the hold timer follows its outline.
- Ripples: each tap sends a ripple out from the button. Pick the ripple's
  outline, its thickness, how long it lingers before it fades, and how many
  rings can stack at once.
- Reward modes: Original (three standing rings that a wave rolls through),
  Ripple, Soft, Spark, Deep, Double, and Wave. Each sets the motion and the
  haptic together.
- Colors: a full color picker with a hue wheel, brightness slider, and hex
  entry for the button, its reward colors, the three ripple colors, and the
  background. Save colors you like to a library under each setting.
- Feel: five haptic strengths, from Gentle to Max, and on supported phones a
  library of over 150 haptic presets to put on a tap or on the timer.
- Random: one press builds a completely new look. Save the ones you love and
  keep them in your library.

Reminders that fit your day

- Calendar nudges: with calendar access, Touchward notices when each event
  ends and nudges you to tap, right away or a few minutes after. The meeting
  is over, the workout is done, come tap.
- Reminders: a schedule you set, so many hours apart between the hours you
  choose.
- Time sensitive delivery on iPhone, so a nudge can reach you in a Focus mode.

Tap from anywhere

- Volume buttons: while the app is open, either volume button counts as a tap.
- A link, touchward://reward, fires a tap when it opens the app. Wire it to
  the Action Button or Back Tap through Shortcuts, or to Quick Tap on Android.
- Apple Watch: the button and its ripples on your wrist, with your reminders
  mirrored, and taps counted alongside the phone.

Private by design

No account and nothing to sign in to. Your settings and your counter stay on
your phone. Calendar data is read only to schedule nudges and is never stored
or sent. The app never asks for your email or your name. If it crashes, an
anonymous report helps us fix it, and you can turn that off.

Touchward is a one time purchase. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no
ads.
