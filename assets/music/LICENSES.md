listing, or the track titles, and none of the audio is a recording or a
melody of anyone else's work.

## Processing

The originals (AAC, about 260 kbps, mastered around -10 LUFS) were level
matched to -16 LUFS integrated with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling and re-encoded to AAC
128 kbps stereo at 44.1 kHz with ffmpeg (`loudnorm`, two pass, linear mode),
which brought the set from 16.0 MB to 7.7 MB. Each piece fades in from and out
to silence, so the app plays them as a shuffled rotation with a crossfade
rather than looping one.
